This is me

👋 Welcome to my personal website!

I completed my master’s degree at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology, Germany. Before that I got my bachelor’s degree at Northeastern University, China. My study interests include deep learning and cognitive systems. I also worked as an intern research assistant at FZI Forschungszentrum Informatik, Karlsruhe on several visual anomaly detection projects.

I currently work as an algorithm engineer at the DEEPRobotics. My primary work and interests focus on the integration of deep reinforcement learning and robotics, but I’m also interested in a wide range of natural sciences, including astronomy and geography, among others.

Make a contribution to Astronomy！

Galaxy Zoo Datasets generated by deep space telescopes and ground-based telescope arrays are often enormous. As a result, many astronomical datasets rely on volunteers to annotate the information, the most well-known of which is the Galaxy Zoo project. It is an example of citizen science as it enlists the help of members of the public to help in scientific research.
