Make a contribution to Astronomy！

Galaxy Zoo Datasets generated by deep space telescopes and ground-based telescope arrays are often enormous. As a result, many astronomical datasets rely on volunteers to annotate the information, the most well-known of which is the Galaxy Zoo project. It is an example of citizen science as it enlists the help of members of the public to help in scientific research. Even if you have no prior experience in astronomy or data labeling, don’t be discouraged! You can always start with the simplest tasks such as Planet Hunters NGTS :) ...
